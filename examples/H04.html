2. Buy after measuring the tank and choosing the fish (buying now is likely to be a mistake)

Filtration (sponge filter) — This goes here because it depends heavily on dimensions, rather than because it is unimportant:

  • Black Easy Flow Sponge Filter (coarse) has no moving parts, does not require replacement filter media, and can be reused for years. Its flow is gentle (friendly to slow-moving fish such as bettas, juvenile shrimp, and fry). Its pricing structure makes it the simplest choice for beginners.
  • However, the page explicitly says that shorter tanks (such as 10-gallon and 20-long tanks) need its size chart checked; a shallow tank may require shortening the uplift tube. The Nano version is labeled "for nano tanks."
  • So the order is: first measure the actual water volume (liters), then check whether your existing air pump has enough flow for this size. If the flow is insufficient, either use a larger sponge or put two small sponges in one tank, rather than simply "buying one large item." I can give you a specific model only after these two details are settled.

Heater — Fluval Aquarium Heater 50 Watts: preset to 76–78°F (about 24–26°C), listed for indoor tanks up to 15 gallons.
