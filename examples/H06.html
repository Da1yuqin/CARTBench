1) Different "pads": the easiest to confuse

NameActual useCan they substitute for each other?

4. You only have one bag: suggested organization

The goal is not equal quantities for mother and baby, but finding items without lengthy rummaging. Pack as follows:

Separate the bag into two transparent / zipped pouches

A. Mother's supplies pouch

Prioritize a small "postpartum-care pouch" in the most accessible place:

  • 2–3 postpartum pads
  • 2–3 pairs of disposable underwear
  • 1 perineal rinse bottle
  • 1 small pack of wipes / toilet paper
  • A few intimate-care wipes if needed

Leave a very small empty pouch called "feeding undecided":

  • 2–4 nursing pads, or none yet
  • A small nipple-cream tube, or none yet
  • No pump accessories unless their use is confirmed

This avoids filling the bag with feeding items prematurely.

B. Baby supplies pouch

Keep only essentials for the birth day and discharge:

  • 2 existing sets of baby clothes
  • 1 swaddle / wrap
  • 3–5 diapers
  • 1 small pack of baby wipes
  • 1 set of existing hat/socks if required by the hospital or weather

Do not fill the only bag with extra diapers, spare clothes, or wash/care products yet.

Place these in the bag's outer section or a personal bag

Keep these separate from clothes/care items:

  • Identity documents, medical-insurance card, prenatal records
  • Phone, charging cable, power bank
  • Drinking cup / straw cup
  • Existing slippers and towels
  • A little cash, tissues, pen
  • Hospital-required documents/forms
