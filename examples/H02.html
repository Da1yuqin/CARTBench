Things you need to confirm before ordering:

  1. Usable internal height of the main compartment—this is the deciding factor. The a6700 body is about 9 cm high, and the 70–350G lens barrel is about 14 cm long. Allowing for the lens hood mounted in reverse, the body thickness, and padding above and below, storing it vertically requires about 23–25 cm of usable internal height. Ask customer service for the "internal dimensions of the camera compartment," rather than looking only at external dimensions.
  2. How to position the lens hood—clarify whether you plan to mount it in reverse or in its normal position, as this can make a difference of more than 5 cm. If the internal height only just accommodates the reversed hood, you will have to reverse the hood every time you put the camera away; you need to be comfortable with that in advance.
  3. Whether the dividers can be removed to create one full-height compartment—if the horizontal partition cannot be completely removed, the body + telephoto lens combination will not fit vertically.
  4. Opening design—confirm whether it opens through the back panel or a top lid; a back-panel opening makes it easier to put in and take out the whole setup with the lens attached.
  5. Official mainland China pricing and sales channels—the search results contain no price information, so use the shop where you will actually order to confirm whether it is within RMB 800, whether it is an officially distributed product, and whether it has a warranty.

If the answer to item 1 is that the usable internal height is insufficient (for example, only a little over 20 cm), let me know, and I will switch to an option confirmed to hold this setup vertically, including the non-backpack styles you have already agreed to consider.
